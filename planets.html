The Eight Planets

Detailed facts: discovery, size, atmosphere, orbital distances, and key characteristics.

Mercury

When DiscoveredKnown since antiquity (visible to the naked eye)
Equatorial Radius~2,440 km (0.38 × Earth)
AtmosphereExtremely thin exosphere (oxygen, sodium, hydrogen, helium, potassium)
Avg. Distance from Sun~57.9 million km (0.39 AU)
Distance from EarthVaries ~77–222 million km
Key CharacteristicsSmallest planet; no moons; extreme temperatures (−173 °C to 427 °C); heavily cratered surface; orbit ~88 Earth days.

Venus

When DiscoveredKnown since antiquity
Equatorial Radius~6,052 km (0.95 × Earth)
AtmosphereVery dense: ~96% CO₂, sulfuric acid clouds; surface pressure ~92× Earth
Avg. Distance from Sun~108.2 million km (0.72 AU)
Distance from EarthVaries ~38–261 million km
Key CharacteristicsHottest planet (~462 °C avg); retrograde rotation; day longer than year; no moons; brightest planet in night sky.

Earth

When DiscoveredN/A – Our home planet
Equatorial Radius~6,371 km
Atmosphere~78% nitrogen, 21% oxygen, 1% argon & other trace gases
Avg. Distance from Sun~149.6 million km (1 AU)
Distance from Earth—
Key CharacteristicsOnly known planet with liquid surface water and life; single moon; ~23.4° axial tilt creating seasons.

Mars

When DiscoveredKnown since antiquity
Equatorial Radius~3,390 km (0.53 × Earth)
AtmosphereThin: mostly CO₂, with N₂ and Ar; surface pressure <1% of Earth
Avg. Distance from Sun~227.9 million km (1.52 AU)
Distance from EarthVaries ~55–400 million km
Key CharacteristicsTwo moons (Phobos & Deimos); polar ice caps; ancient riverbeds; day length ~24.6 hours; year ~687 Earth days.

Jupiter

When DiscoveredKnown since antiquity
Equatorial Radius~69,911 km (~11 × Earth)
AtmosphereMostly hydrogen and helium; swirling cloud bands and Great Red Spot
Avg. Distance from Sun~778.3 million km (5.2 AU)
Distance from EarthVaries ~590–970 million km
Key CharacteristicsLargest planet in solar system; over 90 moons; faint ring system; rapid ~10 hour rotation.

Saturn

When DiscoveredKnown since antiquity
Equatorial Radius~58,232 km (~9.4 × Earth)
AtmosphereMostly hydrogen and helium
Avg. Distance from Sun~1,427 million km (9.5 AU)
Distance from EarthVaries ~1.2–1.7 billion km
Key CharacteristicsSpectacular ice and rock ring system; extensive moon system (Titan is largest); lowest average density planet.

Uranus

When Discovered1781 by William Herschel
Equatorial Radius~25,362 km (~4 × Earth)
AtmosphereHydrogen, helium, and methane (causes pale cyan hue)
Avg. Distance from Sun~2,871 million km (19.2 AU)
Distance from EarthVaries ~2.6–3.2 billion km
Key CharacteristicsIce giant; extreme axial tilt (~98°) causing sideways rotation; 27 known moons; faint dark rings.

Neptune

When Discovered1846 via mathematical prediction
Equatorial Radius~24,622 km (~3.9 × Earth)
AtmosphereHydrogen, helium, and methane
Avg. Distance from Sun~4,498 million km (30 AU)
Distance from EarthVaries ~4.3–4.7 billion km
Key CharacteristicsFarthest major planet; strongest sustained winds in solar system; 14+ moons (Triton is largest); orbit ~165 Earth years.
